About — How it helps / কীভাবে সাহায্য করে

Shobdo makes any text easier to read and hear. Paste or type Bangla or English text, press Play, and follow along word by word.

  • Low vision: large text, high-contrast yellow-on-black theme, and read-aloud voice.
  • Dyslexia: wider letter spacing and roomy line spacing reduce crowding between letters and lines.
  • Elderly readers: big buttons, clear labels, adjustable speed, and simple controls.
  • Bangla speakers: full Bangla voice support (bn-BD) alongside English (en-US).

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no upload, no internet needed after the page loads — your text never leaves your device.
